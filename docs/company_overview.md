## Vision
To build a financially inclusive Ghana where every individual and business — regardless of location or income — can access secure, affordable, and convenient digital financial services.

## Mission
Provide secure, immediate, and affordable financial services to individuals and businesses with limited access to traditional banking, improving financial inclusion for unbanked and underbanked communities.

## Target Market
Urban youth, rural market traders, small-scale farmers, transport operators and commercial drivers, informal micro-enterprises, and other unbanked/underbanked individuals.

---

## Core Products

### MobiKash Smart Wallet
Consumer smartphone app (Android & iOS) — cash deposit via agents, fund storage, P2P transactions, utility bill payments.

### MobiKash Offline USSD Banking (*844#)
Text-based menu for feature phones — instant transfers and balance checks without internet, also usable online.

### MobiKash QuickKredit
Automated micro-lending feature embedded in the wallet — analyzes transaction patterns to instantly issue 14- or 30-day business capital loans, plus overdraft facility.

## Technical Environment

**Cloud Infrastructure:** Fully hosted on AWS, deployed in AWS Africa (Cape Town) Region.

**Database:** Centralized PostgreSQL on Amazon RDS. Sensitive data encrypted at rest and in transit.

**Integration Ecosystem:** Connects to telecom providers (MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo) for USSD/mobile-money, and partner commercial banks for settlement, transfers, and reconciliation.

---

## Regulatory & Compliance Scope

- **Data Protection** — Ghana's Data Protection Act: safeguarding personal data, role-based access, secure storage/processing, retention & disposal practices.
- **Anti-Money Laundering (AML)** — detecting/reporting suspicious transactions, monitoring high-risk activity, maintaining transaction-monitoring records.
- **Know Your Customer (KYC)** — identity verification at onboarding, preventing fraudulent accounts and identity theft, supporting AML due-diligence.
